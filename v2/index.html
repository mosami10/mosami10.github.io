Run 01 Dissertation · individual

F1 brake
cooling.

Three cooling designs for a carbon/carbon disc at 1,500 rpm, compared in ANSYS Fluent with conjugate heat transfer. The scoop duct cooled the disc 109 K more than the dome duct on 65% less mass flow.

Run 02 BAE Systems live brief · group lead

Drone
chassis.

I led the group and ran four ANSYS FEA iterations myself. The final 3D printed chassis was 13.8% lighter at a safety factor of 1.54, then we compression tested printed parts.

Run 03 Individual

HVAC duct.

Six CAD and CFD loops cut the pressure drop of an automotive HVAC duct by 27% and balanced the outlet split to about 50/50. The results fed a Simulink cabin heating model.

03 About

Decisions,
backed by data.

Simulation without engineering judgement is only pictures. I check a result before I trust it: mesh studies, y+ checks, and lab tests against the FEA where I can get them.

My strongest work is the full loop. Model it, test it on screen, build it, then test it for real. A three year mechanical engineering diploma in Mumbai (83%, Distinction) gave me the workshop base. The BEng at Anglia Ruskin added CFD and FEA on top.

I finished my final year while working night shifts at a parcel depot, so I know how to plan around fixed hours and still deliver.
