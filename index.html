01 — About

Decisions,
defended with data.

Simulation without engineering judgement is just pictures. Every project here makes a call — and backs it with numbers.

Nine projects across automotive, aerospace and energy. The flagship: an F1 brake cooling dissertation: three configurations compared in ANSYS Fluent with conjugate heat transfer, resolving a 712 K peak disc temperature. The scoop duct (B) cooled the disc 109 K more than the dome duct on 65% less mass flow. The unducted baseline hit thermal runaway.

But the work doesn't stop at the screen. The same portfolio includes workshop-built prototypes, tensile and vibration lab testing, 3D-printed structural parts, and Arduino-controlled machines — from Mumbai diploma (First Class with Distinction) to a BEng (Hons) at Anglia Ruskin, graduated 2026 with a 2:1.

That's five years of mechanical engineering training: a three-year technical diploma in Mumbai (83%, Distinction) built the hands-on base — engineering drawing, machine design, thermal and fluid engineering, solid modelling — before the BEng added high-fidelity simulation on top.
